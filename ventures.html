In development · Internal capital allocation

NoirShield Capital

Internal only

NoirShield Capital is being developed as the group’s dedicated internal capital-allocation and long-term investment vehicle. Its planned role is to separate permanent investment capital from higher-liability operating activities and systematically reinvest excess distributable business capital across diversified public equities, fixed income, liquidity reserves, and other internally approved assets under defined risk and concentration limits.

NoirShield Capital is intended only for the group’s own internal capital and is not offered to outside clients or third-party investors.

Long-term regulated objective

NoirShield Captive Insurance Company

Future

A future affiliated risk-financing company whose purpose would be to finance legitimate, measurable insurance exposures—not to serve as the group’s general investment vehicle. Any captive would require independent actuarial feasibility, risk transfer and distribution, defensible premium pricing, sufficient capitalization, domicile selection, claims administration, regulatory approval, and insurance licensing.

Required disclosure: NoirShield Captive Insurance Company is a future planning objective. It is not currently formed or licensed, does not operate as an insurer, and does not offer insurance products or services. It does not issue policies or collect insurance premiums.

Strategy flow

Two branches. Two different jobs.

Capital compounds excess resources. A future captive would finance legitimate insurance risk. They are intentionally separate.

Operating and capital branch

Real assets→Operating cash flow→Reserves and reinvestment→NoirShield Capital→Long-term compounding

Risk-financing branch

Measurable business risk→Actuarial analysis→Feasibility and regulation→Potential future captive

Operating doctrine

Structure before scale

The platform is designed to preserve clear boundaries between ownership, operations, permanent investment capital, and regulated insurance risk.

  • Real assets and operations: produce external cash flow through legitimate rental and operating activity.
  • Holdings: organizes strategic control and long-term ownership interests.
  • Management: coordinates active oversight, administration, vendors, improvements, and business infrastructure.
  • Capital: compounds internal excess capital under defined allocation, liquidity, and concentration policies.
  • Captive: remains a separate future risk-financing branch governed by actuarial and regulatory requirements.
